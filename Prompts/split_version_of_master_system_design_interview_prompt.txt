
Response of master prompt would easily exceed 100,000+ words, which is well beyond the 
maximum response size any single ChatGPT message can generate.
That's why split it into a small number of logically complete parts.


Why this split works

This four-part structure follows the natural flow of a Staff-level system design interview:

Part 1: Define the problem and design the data model (requirements, estimates, APIs, database).
Part 2: Build the architecture incrementally from a simple request flow to a production-ready distributed system.
Part 3: Demonstrate senior engineering judgment through architectural decisions, failure handling, scalability, security, and trade-offs.
Part 4: Prepare for the discussion portion with deep follow-up questions, a presentation timeline, and a concise cheat sheet.

Each part is self-contained, avoids repetition, and together they form one cohesive, interview-ready design document.
